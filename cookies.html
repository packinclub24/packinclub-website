1. What Are Cookies?

Cookies are small text files stored on your device when you visit a website. They help websites function correctly, remember your preferences, and understand how visitors use the site. We also use similar browser storage mechanisms (like localStorage) for the same purposes.

2. Essential Browser Storage

We use a small amount of essential browser storage that is always active and cannot be switched off, because the Site cannot function properly without it. This does not track you across other websites and does not require consent under applicable law.

NamePurposeDuration

3. Google Analytics & Analytics Cookies

We use Google Analytics (GA4) to understand how visitors use the Site — pages viewed, time on site, traffic sources, and general device/browser information. Google Analytics sets its own cookies (such as _ga and _ga_*) to distinguish visitors and sessions.

These cookies are optional. The Google Analytics script is only loaded onto the Site after you click "Accept" in the cookie banner or in the preferences panel below. If you decline or take no action, no Google Analytics script loads and no analytics cookies are set.

NamePurposeDuration
_gaDistinguishes unique visitorsUp to 2 years
_ga_*Persists session state for GA4 propertyUp to 2 years

4. Google Maps & Third-Party Content

Our Contact page embeds a Google Maps view of our office location. Google Maps may set its own cookies or collect data when the map is loaded or interacted with, governed by Google's own privacy and cookie policies, independent of our consent banner. We use Google Maps for the essential purpose of showing our location, and this is loaded regardless of your analytics preference, since it does not involve analytics tracking.
