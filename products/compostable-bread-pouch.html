Compostable Bread Pouch

Keeps loaves fresh and breathable, then composts along with the crumbs.

Overview

A breathable bread and bakery pouch that keeps loaves fresh without trapping moisture and going soggy. Crystal clear for shelf appeal, food-contact safe, and fully compostable, so day-old bread and its bag can go into the same green bin.

Key features

Breathable, keeps crust crisp
High-clarity for shelf display
Food-contact safe
Re-closable or heat-sealable formats
Composts with bakery waste

Applications

Artisan and retail bakeries Supermarket in-store bakery Cafés and patisseries Home and cloud bakeries
