Who uses compostable pet poop bags

Pet waste bags are used by pet stores and vet clinics, dog-walking and pet services, parks and public spaces, and pet-owner retail and D2C brands. They are leak-proof and odour-resistant, and easy to open and tie.

The bags come on tear-off rolls that fit standard dispensers and leash clips, packed in retail cartons.

SpecificationRange

The bag size is 9"×13", with 15 to 20 bags per roll. The rolls are packed in retail cartons, which suits shelf display in a pet store. For dispenser stations in parks, tell us the dispenser model so that the core size can be matched.

How to choose the right thickness

Bags are made in 18 to 25 microns. The film is thin enough to open easily with one hand, and strong enough to hold the contents without leaking. If you supply public dispensers that are exposed to heat and moisture, store rolls in a cool, dry place and trial a batch before a full rollout.

Disposal and storage

Do not add pet waste to a compost heap used for food crops. Check whether your local composting facility accepts pet waste; if it does not, dispose of the bag in general waste as per local rules. Our specification states that the bag returns to soil within 240 days in industrial composting. Store unused rolls in a cool, dry place; shelf life is 12 months from the date of manufacture.
