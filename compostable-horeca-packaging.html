Compostable Food Packaging for Hotels, Restaurants and Cafés

Compostable food packaging: aqueous coated paper cups, pouches, bread bags, cling film and bamboo tissue for restaurants, cafés and cloud kitchens. Bulk supply from Delhi.

Get a quote →

A café’s packaging is part of its brand. Packin Club supplies compostable food packaging for hotels, restaurants, cafés, bakeries and cloud kitchens: aqueous coated paper cups for tea and coffee, pouches, bread pouches, hot-liquid pouches, cling film and bamboo tissue.

Our disposable paper cups use a water-based aqueous coating instead of a plastic lining, and can be printed with your design as custom paper cups. Small tea cups and larger coffee cups are available in a range of sizes.

Who buys horeca packaging

Cafés and tea stalls, restaurant and hotel groups, QSR chains, bakeries, caterers, cloud kitchens and event organisers who want compostable food packaging that looks good and meets food-contact needs.

How to choose the right specification

Choose cups by size and hot or cold use, pouches by the product and fill temperature, and film by the food you wrap. Tell us your volumes and print needs, and we will recommend a specification and send samples.

Certified, made in bulk, supplied across India

Every Packin Club product is made to IS 17088, is CPCB certified, and returns to soil within 240 days under industrial composting conditions. We make to order with a minimum of 100 kg per specification, and ship from Delhi to Delhi NCR, Noida, Gurugram, Ludhiana, Mumbai, Pune, Chennai and Coimbatore and other cities.

Frequently asked questions

Are aqueous coated paper cups compostable?

Aqueous coated cups use a water-based coating rather than a plastic lining. Whether they compost depends on the coating and the facility, so we supply the certification for each product. See our article on whether aqueous coated paper cups are actually compostable.

Can I get custom paper cups with my own print?

Yes. Cups can be printed to your artwork on made-to-order runs. Share your size, quantity and artwork for a quote.

Do you make a compostable pouch bag or mini pouch for food?

Yes. We make compostable pouches in several sizes, including bread pouches and hot-liquid pouches. Tell us the food, temperature and weight.

Can you supply restaurants and cafés in Mumbai, Pune, Chennai and Coimbatore?

Yes. We ship from Delhi to Delhi NCR, Noida, Gurugram, Ludhiana, Mumbai, Pune, Chennai and Coimbatore and other cities across India.
