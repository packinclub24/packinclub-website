Who buys bags on roll packaging

Supermarkets, kirana and grocery chains, vegetable and fruit sellers, hotels and housekeeping contractors, offices, housing societies and distributors who buy compostable trash bags and counter bags in volume.

How to choose the right specification

Pick the roll by use: garbage bags by bin size and load, carry bags by handle style and weight, grocery bags by what goes in them. We can print your brand on carry and grocery rolls with food-grade inks.

Certified, made in bulk, supplied across India

Every Packin Club product is made to IS 17088, is CPCB certified, and returns to soil within 240 days under industrial composting conditions. We make to order with a minimum of 100 kg per specification, and ship from Delhi to Delhi NCR, Noida, Gurugram, Ludhiana, Mumbai, Pune, Chennai and Coimbatore and other cities.

Frequently asked questions

What are the best compostable trash bags for home and office use?

Look for bags that state IS 17088 certification, a tested load rating and a roll format that suits your bin size. Our compostable garbage bags on roll are made for that, and we can advise on size and thickness.

Are compostable garbage bags strong enough for wet waste?

Yes, within the stated load. Compostable bags perform best for dry and wet household waste that is collected and composted soon after. Store rolls in a cool, dry place and use them within the 12-month shelf life.

Can you print our logo on carry bags on roll?

Yes. We print up to 8 colours with food-grade flexographic inks on made-to-order rolls.

Do you deliver compostable bags on roll to Mumbai, Pune, Chennai and Coimbatore?

Yes. We ship from Delhi to Delhi NCR, Noida, Gurugram, Ludhiana, Mumbai, Pune, Chennai and Coimbatore and other cities across India.
