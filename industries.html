FAQ

Frequently asked questions

Which industries do you supply?+
E-commerce and D2C, food service and HORECA, cloud kitchens and delivery, retail and fashion, grocery and supermarkets, agriculture and nursery, healthcare and hospitals, industrial and logistics, and temples and religious organisations.
Can you match the plastic items I use today?+
Yes. We map each plastic item on your line by size, volume and function, pair it to a certified compostable format, or tool it to your exact spec where needed. Samples go out for you to test on your real packing lines before any commitment.
What do restaurants and cloud kitchens use?+
Typical formats are hot liquid pouches rated up to 95°C, bread pouches, cling film, aqueous coated paper cups and bamboo tissue. Test any format against your menu and delivery routes before switching. Compostable packaging for restaurants and cloud kitchens.
Do you supply packaging for healthcare?+
We supply colour-coded compostable bags for segregating non-hazardous clinical waste, made for BMWM 2016 colour segregation. Acceptance depends on your state pollution control board and treatment provider, so confirm your requirement first. Used bags containing biomedical waste follow your facility’s biomedical waste protocol and are not composted.
What do farms and nurseries use?+
Compostable mulch film that is tilled into the soil at harvest, and nursery bags that are planted whole with the seedling. Compostable mulch film: a practical guide for Indian farmers.
My industry is not listed. Can you still help?+
If you ship it, we can pack it. Tell us what you move and how, and we will match each format to a certified compostable equivalent or tool it to your exact spec. Start on the contact page.
