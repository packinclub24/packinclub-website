About Vikas

Vikas Kumar Jha is an Indian entrepreneur and founder of Packin Club, a sustainable packaging brand focused on replacing conventional single-use plastic packaging with compostable solutions made from plant-based and other compostable materials. With a vision to make responsible packaging a practical choice for businesses, Packin Club offers an expanding range of packaging solutions designed to serve everyday commercial and industrial needs while reducing dependence on conventional plastics.

Holding an MBA in Marketing and Operations, Vikas brings experience across entrepreneurship, business development, sales, marketing, and operations. His professional journey spans multiple industries, including ventures in grocery distribution and digital marketing, giving him firsthand exposure to business challenges, market dynamics, and the realities of building and scaling a company.

At Packin Club, he leads business strategy, product portfolio development, sourcing, pricing, partnerships, and market expansion. His focus is on building a trusted Indian packaging brand that combines environmental responsibility with product functionality, regulatory compliance, and commercial viability.

His approach to entrepreneurship is shaped by a combination of strategic thinking and hands-on execution, with an emphasis on understanding market needs, building reliable business relationships, and creating solutions that deliver long-term value. Through Packin Club, he aims to contribute to the wider adoption of compostable packaging while building a competitive Indian brand with ambitions beyond domestic markets.
